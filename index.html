FrameForge

A personal desktop app for producing videos in DaVinci Resolve from a script, a voiceover and a library of the owner's own clips.

What it does with YouTube

FrameForge has a section called My Channel. When the owner of a YouTube channel connects it, FrameForge reads how that channel's videos have performed, such as views, watch time and where viewers stop watching, so that the channel's next videos can be edited better.

Access is read-only. FrameForge cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.

Who it is for

FrameForge runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a website or an online service, and there is nothing to sign up for here.
